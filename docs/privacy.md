# Privacy Policy

**Android application:** Litteratim: A Game of Words  

**Developer:** LABraga Games  

**Last updated:** 2026-10-09

## INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE

This Privacy Policy transparently describes the data processing associated with the mobile application Litteratim: A Game of Words, developed by LABraga Games (hereinafter the "Application" or "Game").

This Policy was prepared taking into account Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation — "GDPR") and the applicable Google Play requirements and policies.

The Application is intended for teenagers and adults. It was not intentionally designed for or directed at children.

The Application was developed without its own servers and with a predominantly client-side architecture. The Developer does not keep its own database of users' personal data on its own servers.

However, to enable the distribution, operation, monetisation and purchase processing of the Application, third-party services are used that may process certain technical data, device identifiers or other information, as described in this Policy and in the privacy policies of the respective providers.

## DATA COLLECTION AND PROCESSING

The Game does not ask the user for data such as an email address, telephone number, postal address or precise location, nor does it have its own infrastructure to store a database with such information.

In games between several people on the same device, the user may, if they wish, enter names or nicknames to identify the players. These names are optional, are used only to display them during the Game and are stored exclusively on the device itself.

The Application does not send this data to the Developer's own servers nor use it to create its own user profiles. However, the third-party SDKs and services integrated into the Application may collect and process certain data according to their respective purposes, settings and policies.

Depending on the service used, this information may include, among others:

- technical or advertising identifiers of the device;
- IP address;
- information about the device and operating system;
- technical information needed to deliver and measure advertising;
- technical information related to purchases made through Google Play Billing.

Processing carried out by these providers is performed in accordance with their applicable policies, terms and settings.

The Developer does not use the above data to create its own database of users' personal profiles.

## PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

Technical data that may be processed by the third-party services integrated into the Application may be used, as applicable, to:

- enable the technical operation of the Application and associated services;
- deliver, manage and measure advertising;
- process and validate purchases made through Google Play;
- prevent fraud, abuse and misuse of the services;
- ensure the security and operation of the third-party services.

The Developer does not use this data to create its own usage profiles or to keep a personal history of players' activity.

## DATA STORED ONLY ON THE DEVICE

For the Game to work, the following information is stored locally on the device, and only there:

- the game in progress, so that it can be resumed;
- records, statistics and achievements;
- virtual currency, helps and styles obtained;
- preferences (sound, music, contrast, styles, interface and dictionary languages and game options);
- player names entered by the user, if any.

This information is not sent to the Developer's servers nor shared with third parties. It is deleted when the user clears the Application's data or uninstalls it.

If Android backup is enabled on the device, the operating system may include this data in the backup of the user's Google account, under the terms defined by Google.

## NO ANALYTICS OR CRASH REPORTING TOOLS

The Application does not integrate external statistical analysis tools ("Analytics") or external automated crash reporting tools ("Crash Reporting").

The Developer does not, on its own initiative, collect or keep data intended to create usage profiles, analyse players' behaviour or keep a history of use of the Application.

The third-party services integrated into the Application may, however, carry out their own technical processing necessary to provide their services, in accordance with their privacy policies.

## THIRD-PARTY SERVICES AND PROVIDERS

The Application uses third-party services for certain features. These services may process technical data or other data necessary to provide their services.

The main services used are:

- Google Play Services — services needed for the Application to work in the Android ecosystem and for integration with Google Play.
- Google AdMob — delivery, management and measurement of the advertising shown in the Application.
- Google Play Billing — processing of purchases of digital products made within the Application.

Each provider may process data in accordance with its own privacy policies and terms of service. Users are advised to consult those policies for additional information about the processing carried out directly by those providers.

## INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

The third-party services used by the Application may process data on servers located outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Where such processing involves international transfers of personal data, the respective providers must use the transfer mechanisms provided for in applicable law, such as adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission or other legally recognised mechanisms, as applicable.

For specific information about the transfers carried out by each provider, the user should consult the respective privacy policies.

## LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING

Where applicable to the processing of personal data, it may be based on the following legal bases provided for in Article 6 of the GDPR:

- Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — in particular where consent is legally required for certain uses of data for personalised advertising.
- Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — where necessary to provide the digital products purchased by the user through the Google Play purchase system.
- Compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)) — where processing is necessary to comply with an applicable legal obligation.
- Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — where legally applicable, in particular for certain processing necessary for security, fraud prevention, technical operation and protection of the services.

The specific legal basis applicable to a given processing operation may depend on the nature of the service, the user's location and the applicable consent settings.

## ADVERTISING AND CONSENT IN THE EUROPEAN UNION, EEA, UNITED KINGDOM AND SWITZERLAND

The Game shows advertising through Google AdMob to fund the development and free availability of the Application.

In regions where applicable law requires consent for certain forms of personalised advertising, the Application uses the consent management mechanism provided by Google, including the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, where applicable:

- a consent message will be shown through Google's consent management mechanism;
- the user will be able to manage the options regarding the use of data for personalised advertising and other applicable purposes;
- where consent to personalised advertising is not given, non-personalised ads may be shown, in accordance with the applicable Google AdMob settings and policies;
- where permitted by applicable law, the user may later change or withdraw consent through the options provided by the Application.

The availability, format and content of the consent messages may be updated by Google to reflect changes in its services or in applicable law.

## YOUR CONSENT

Where processing is based on consent, it will be requested through the appropriate mechanisms, including, where applicable, Google's consent management system.

The user may withdraw consent at any time through the mechanisms provided for that purpose.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out on the basis of consent before its withdrawal.

Withdrawing consent may affect the personalisation of the advertising shown in the Application, but should not prevent access to the Game's essential features, unless this is technically or legally necessary.

## IN-APP PURCHASES AND FINANCIAL DATA

The Game allows purchases of digital products within the Application.

The Developer does not collect, view or store payment card data, bank card numbers, banking credentials or other financial data needed to make the payment.

Transactions are processed by Google through the official Google Play Billing system.

The Application receives only the technical information needed to check the status of the purchase and to make the purchased digital product available to the user.

Purchases and the related billing data are also subject to Google Play's terms and privacy policies.

- Right to restriction of processing — to request, in the cases provided for by law, the restriction of the processing of your data;
